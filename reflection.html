Reflection

The Vision

This portfolio was designed to feel like a well-loved sketchbook — warm, textured, and personal. I wanted the visual identity to reflect who I am: a designer, sewist, Navy veteran, mom, and creative problem-solver. The coffee stains, handwritten headings, and warm parchment tones aren't just decoration — they're an extension of my process.

Design Choices

I chose Moon Dance for headings because it has the hand-lettered feel I wanted — as if each title were written with a fountain pen on a page of my notebook. Montserrat provides a clean, readable counterpoint for body text, keeping the information clear and accessible.

The color palette draws from warm, earthy tones: cream backgrounds, espresso brown navigation and footer, and muted accent colors (teal, plum, sage, burnt orange) that give each project card its own personality without overwhelming the overall layout.

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was reconciling what I imagined in my head with what actually worked in the browser. The mockup always looks perfect — but CSS has a way of reminding you that the web is a flexible, unpredictable medium. Learning to embrace that misalignment rather than fight it was a valuable lesson.

Making the site responsive was another hurdle. What looks great on a 1440px screen needs to be rethought for a 375px phone. I used CSS Grid for the project cards (which collapse to single-column on mobile) and Flexbox for the about section and navigation to keep things flexible.

What I Learned

This project reinforced the value of starting with a strong foundation. Semantic HTML, organized CSS with variables, and a clear visual hierarchy made the build process smoother and the end result more maintainable. I also gained a deeper appreciation for the little details — hover states, transitions, link styling — that make a site feel polished and intentional.

Final Thoughts

This portfolio is a snapshot of where I am right now as a designer. It's not perfect, but it's mine — built by hand, fueled by caffeine, and held together with a healthy dose of organized chaos.
