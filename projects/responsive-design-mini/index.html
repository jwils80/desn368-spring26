Micro Guide: Mom's Potential Quiet Spots

Three places to read, sketch, or reset between demands.

Is Mom Hiding?

Moms sometimes just need a minute, and sometimes they need longer than that. Here are some ideas for hiding spots...I mean rest spots for mom.

Bathroom

The Master Bath

Place number one is the bathroom. Most of the time, your minion won't bother you in the bathroom. Bonus points if you are taking a bubble bath.

Vehicle

The Driveway

This is the ideal spot for hiding...I mean chilling after you get home from taxi duties, and/or errands. Park in the driveway, finish listening to your song, and catch the next one, and take a few breaths before you venture into whatever shit show the minions and ring leader caused. Bonus points if you just sit in the quiet for a few minutes.

The Living Room

Don't Forget the Headphones

One of the best places to chill, is in plain sight. You can be right in the middle of everything, and still off in your own little world...as long as you have your headphones. Remember, when the mini yous talk to you, look at them and nod every once in a while so they 'know' you're listening.
